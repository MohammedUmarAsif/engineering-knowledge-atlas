# Research Criticism Lens

For every paper, ask:

- What precise claim is made?
- What would falsify it?
- Is the method appropriate?
- Are baselines and ablations adequate?
- Is the dataset representative or contaminated?
- Are the metrics valid for the claim?
- Are effect sizes meaningful?
- Can the result be reproduced?
- What limitations or negative results are omitted?
- Does the evidence justify practical adoption?
