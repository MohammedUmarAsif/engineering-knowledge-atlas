# Senior Engineering Lens

Apply these questions after every technical module:

1. What user or business problem is being solved?
2. What assumptions make the design valid?
3. What is the simplest adequate design?
4. What fails first, and how will anyone know?
5. Which invariants must always hold?
6. How are security and privacy boundaries enforced?
7. What are the latency, capacity, and cost limits?
8. How will the system be tested and observed?
9. How can it be migrated, rolled back, or removed?
10. Which teams own each boundary and failure?
11. What evidence supports the choice?
12. What new evidence would change it?

Senior thinking is not maximal complexity. It is making consequences visible and choosing deliberately.
