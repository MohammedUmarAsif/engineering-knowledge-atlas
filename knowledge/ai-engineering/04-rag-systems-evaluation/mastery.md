# RAG Systems Mastery Review

## Explain in 30 seconds

- RAG
- Query rewriting
- Adaptive retrieval
- Corrective RAG
- Self-RAG
- RAPTOR
- GraphRAG
- Groundedness
- Citation support

## Compare in three minutes

1. RAG versus long context, tools, fine-tuning, and database queries.
2. Baseline hybrid RAG versus GraphRAG.
3. Retrieval evaluation versus answer evaluation.
4. Corrective RAG versus a conventional reranker and failure policy.
5. Human versus model-based evaluation.

## Architecture defence

Design a legal-policy assistant, then defend:

- corpus authority and temporal validity;
- ingestion and provenance;
- permissions;
- query rewriting/routing;
- retrieval and reranking;
- conflict handling;
- claim/citation contract;
- no-answer policy;
- layered evaluation;
- security and deletion;
- latency, cost, caching, rollout, and incidents.

## PhD-level test

Choose one advanced RAG method and state:

1. the exact baseline failure;
2. its causal mechanism;
3. hidden assumptions;
4. falsifiable hypothesis;
5. datasets and metrics;
6. confounders;
7. compute/operational cost;
8. negative result that would make you reject it.

## Exit standard

You can locate RAG failures by layer, defend simplicity, design a controlled evaluation, read a method paper critically, and explain which result would change your architecture.
