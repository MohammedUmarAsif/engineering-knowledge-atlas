# Curriculum Standard

## Design basis

The atlas uses:

- ACM/IEEE-CS/AAAI CS2023 for durable computer-science coverage;
- IEEE SWEBOK v4.0a for professional software-engineering coverage;
- selected current Harvard, MIT and Oxford course material for academic depth and sequencing, with its influence and limits recorded in the [academic source map](academic-source-map.md);
- official specifications and product documentation for changing implementation details;
- peer-reviewed research and engineering postmortems for evidence and failure analysis.

It deliberately does not reproduce an undergraduate degree. Existing BSc material is tested through diagnostics and revisited only where understanding is weak.

All modules also follow the [teaching and depth standard](teaching-and-depth-standard.md): intuition must become precise mechanism, tools must follow problems, and modern claims must be dated and sourced.

## Module contract

Every complete module must contain:

1. Purpose
2. Prerequisites
3. Diagnostic and explicit skip destination
4. Learning outcomes
5. Mental model
6. Mechanism
7. Trade-offs
8. Failure modes
9. Production implications
10. Senior reasoning questions
11. Interview questions
12. Primary and further sources
13. Connections and next module
14. Last-reviewed date
15. Misconceptions and corrections
16. Cross-domain applications where genuinely relevant
17. Current tools and selected repositories
18. Research frontier and open questions

When implementation clarifies the concept, the module must also follow the [language and code policy](language-and-code-policy.md): Python first for AI and C++ first for games, with paired examples where the comparison is genuinely useful.

## Depth labels

- **L0 Orientation:** vocabulary and landscape
- **L1 Foundation:** mechanism and basic reasoning
- **L2 Application:** component interaction
- **L3 Production:** reliability, security, and operation
- **L4 Senior:** architecture and organizational trade-offs
- **L5 Research:** evidence, limitations, and open problems
